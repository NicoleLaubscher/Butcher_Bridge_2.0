Decision-model specification

Every recommendation is traceable to an explicit rule.

The model separates calculation logic from Flask and MySQL. This makes the formulas independently testable and prevents the interface from hiding assumptions.

1

Compatible demand

Only committed demand submitted before the order-window close is included. Product must match and required dates must fall inside the configured compatibility window.

QG = Σ qi
2

Supplier feasibility

Product, whole-order capacity, service area, lead time, price-tier eligibility, expected fill rate and on-time performance are checked before ranking.

3

Landed cost

Purchase price alone is never used as the decision metric. Fixed fulfilment, variable fulfilment and handling are included.

Cs = [Ps(Q)Q + Tfixed + TvariableQ + Hs] / Q
4

Capacity allocation

Retailer orders remain whole. The exact allocator tests feasible supplier assignments, respects capacity and applies price tiers to the quantity allocated to each supplier.

5

Shared logistics

Fixed logistics and handling costs are allocated in proportion to each retailer’s kilograms. The same rule is shown in retailer-level output.

6

Acceptance rule

Aggregation is accepted only when it meets every constraint and its total landed cost is lower than the best feasible individual option. Equality or a worse result causes rejection.
