# Primary-data and usability evaluation plan

## Primary-data collection fields

| Source | Minimum observation | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| Each retailer | Order quantity, frequency, price/kg, logistics/handling cost, trip distance, trip count, fill rate, on-time rate | Invoice/receipt, trip log, date and respondent code |
| Each supplier | Dated price tiers, available capacity, lead time, service areas, fixed/variable fulfilment, expected service | Written quotation and validity date |
| Completed order | Ordered/delivered kg, due/actual date, total and logistics cost, trips, distance, exceptions | Delivery note, invoice and dispatch/receipt timestamps |

Use anonymised participant codes in demonstration screenshots. Do not commit personal details or confidential quotations to GitHub.

## Usability protocol

Ask at least one intended user from each available role to complete: (1) enter demand, (2) interpret a blocked supplier, (3) explain why a recommendation was accepted/rejected, and (4) record an actual outcome. Record task success, completion time, errors, assistance required and a 1–5 ease score.

Do not report this as completed user testing until real participants have performed it. Add dated results below after the session.
